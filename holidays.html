Company-wide holidays

Sundays are off and Saturdays are working days. These public holidays are shaded on the Gantt chart. When a leave starts on one of them or on a Sunday, the day count starts on the next working day.

Eid dates depend on moon sighting and may move by a day or two. If a date changes, update it in data.js.
